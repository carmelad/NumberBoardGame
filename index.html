Rules
  • Match equal numbers or numbers totaling 10.
  • Connect along a row, column, diagonal, or reading order. Intermediate cells must be empty.
  • Earn 2 points plus 1 per empty cell crossed.
  • In Versus AI, successful moves alternate turns. Each player receives their own points and bonuses.
  • Select 2–4 numbers anywhere on the board.
  • Use each selected number exactly once to reach the target. Multiplication and division follow normal precedence.
  • Parentheses and fractional intermediate results are allowed. Division by zero is invalid.
  • Earn 2 points per number used in a correct expression.
  • The target remains fixed throughout each level.
  • Faded numbers are empty and do not block moves.
  • Cleared rows disappear; columns never collapse.
  • Earn 10 bonus points per cleared row and 150 per cleared board.
  • Each level provides 3 hints and 3 additions. Additions are shared in Versus AI and do not consume a turn.
  • Scores and total time carry forward. Level time, hints, and additions reset on each new level.
  • No valid moves and no additions means game over.
  • Back to Menu ends the current session.
